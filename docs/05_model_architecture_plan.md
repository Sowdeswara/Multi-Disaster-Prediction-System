**Key points of the planned architecture**:
- A **shared temporal encoder** will learn a common representation of the multi‑timescale environmental state.
- **Hazard‑specific heads** (Flood, Fire, Landslide) will be attached to the shared encoder to produce risk scores for each hazard.
- The **Flood head** is the first model component that will be developed; fire and landslide heads are scoped for future work.
- The design enables **reuse of learned representations**, reducing redundant computation across hazards and supporting incremental addition of new hazard heads.
- The architecture is intended for **edge deployment** on resource‑constrained smartphones, with a compact feature space generated by the hierarchical temporal‑memory layer.

*No model has been trained yet – this document describes the intended structure.*
